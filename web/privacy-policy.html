Privacy Policy

OmniToolkit • Last updated: October 2, 2026 • Version 2.0 • Applies to OmniToolkit on Windows, the Web (PWA), and any other platform where it is published.

1. Who We Are & What This Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how the OmniToolkit application ("OmniToolkit", "we", "us") handles information when you use our daily utility suite: Calendar & Live Clock (including the optional Weather card), 3D Calculator & Unit Converter, Radio/TV Explorer, US Location & Area Code Lookup, and Password Generator.

Summary

  • No account, and no servers of ours: OmniToolkit has no backend. Your notes, favorites, settings and generated passwords never leave your device unless you contact us yourself.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no sale of data.
  • A few third-party network requests do happen — only when you use Weather (your coordinates go to MET Norway), browse or play Radio (station directory and stream servers), or the web app loads its static assets. All of them are listed in section 4.
  • Location is only requested when you tap "Show weather" — never automatically at startup.

Publisher / contact: BTIM Development Team / OmniToolkit — support@omnitoolkit.app. Your use of the app is also governed by our Terms of Use.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

OmniToolkit is offline-first. The following information is created by you or generated locally and stays on your device:

  • Calendar notes — stored in a local SQLite database.
  • App settings (theme, 24-hour clock, and a temporary weather cache) — stored in local app preferences.
  • Radio favorites — stored locally in SQLite / local preferences.
  • ZIP / area code lookups, holiday calendar, bundled station directory — imported from datasets shipped with the app into local SQLite; searches run entirely on-device.
  • Password Generator — generated passwords and history exist only in memory (RAM) for the current session and are discarded when you close the module or the app. Nothing is written to disk and nothing is synced.
  • Calculator history — session memory only; cleared when you press AC or restart the app.

There is no cloud sync and no cloud backup. Deleting your notes, favorites or settings in the app, clearing site data (web version), or uninstalling the app permanently removes this information.

3. Location & Weather

The Calendar screen contains an optional Weather card. Weather is off until you turn it on:

  • Nothing happens on startup. Only when you tap "Show weather" does the app ask your operating system or browser for location permission.
  • If you grant it, your coordinates (rounded to 4 decimal places) and altitude are sent directly from your device over HTTPS to the Met Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) locationforecast API, together with an identifying OmniToolkit/1.0 User-Agent and your IP address (inherent to any HTTP request).
  • The response (temperature, conditions, alerts) is cached locally for about 45 minutes under a key containing the same rounded coordinates.
  • If you deny permission, or never tap the button, no location is requested and no coordinates are sent — the rest of the app works exactly the same.
  • OmniToolkit never receives your coordinates: the app is fully client-side, so the request goes from your device to MET Norway only. We operate no server that could observe it.

You can revoke location permission at any time in your OS or browser settings. For the web version, geolocation access is restricted to OmniToolkit's own origin through the Permissions-Policy header, so no other site or embed can trigger it.

5. What We Do Not Collect

  • No account / registration: no name, email, phone or payment information is gathered inside the app (authentication, cloud sync and payment features have been removed from OmniToolkit).
  • No analytics or telemetry: no usage tracking, session recording, crash reporting, or profiling.
  • No advertising: no ad networks, advertising IDs, or promotional SDKs.
  • No tracking cookies and no device fingerprinting.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell, rent, or trade data with anyone.
  • No contact details unless you choose to email us.

6. US ZIP, City, County & Area Code Lookup

The US Location & Area Code Lookup module operates 100% offline using bundled local datasets (curated US ZIP records and NANPA area code data). Searches for ZIP codes, ZIP+4 inputs, city names, counties, and area codes are executed locally against an offline in-memory/SQLite database with zero external API calls, third-party requests, or location tracking.

The bundled dataset is a static snapshot provided for convenience; it may be incomplete or out of date and must not be relied upon for emergency, legal, or navigation purposes.

7. Local Storage, Cookies & Service Worker (Web Version)

  • OmniToolkit sets no tracking cookies.
  • The web version keeps your data in localStorage, IndexedDB (SQLite), and local app preferences inside your browser.
  • A service worker caches the app's own same-origin files so it starts and works offline. It only handles files served from the OmniToolkit origin and never intercepts or caches third-party traffic.
  • The app shell is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub processes standard web-server request logs when you load it (see section 4).

To erase everything: use your browser's "Clear site data" for the OmniToolkit site, or clear the app's local data / uninstall it on desktop.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

  • On-device data (notes, favorites, settings): kept until you delete it in the app or remove the app/site data. There are no server-side copies.
  • Weather cache: expires automatically after about 45 minutes.
  • Passwords and calculator history: not retained beyond the current session.
  • Email you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you and to document your request.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Where applicable law gives you rights over personal data (for example the GDPR/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may request:

  • access to, or a copy of, the personal data we hold about you,
  • correction or deletion of that data,
  • portability, restriction of, or objection to processing,

Because OmniToolkit processes your data on your own device, most of these rights can be exercised directly by deleting the data in the app or clearing site data. For anything else, email support@omnitoolkit.app with the subject "Privacy Request". We respond within the time required by law and free of charge. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

10. Children's Privacy

OmniToolkit is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age set by your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

  • Local-only architecture: the most sensitive data (calendar notes, generated passwords) never leaves your device.
  • HTTPS-only connections; Content-Security-Policy, Permissions-Policy, Referrer-Policy, and service-worker hardening on the web build.
  • Radio stream URLs are validated (HTTP/HTTPS only, with localhost and private IP addresses blocked in release builds).
  • Passwords are generated with cryptographically secure randomness (Random.secure()).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Hosting & International Transfers

The web app is a static site served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). GitHub and the third parties listed in section 4 may process data in countries other than your own. Where personal data is transferred internationally, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) and their own terms.

13. Third-Party Links & Content

Radio streams, station logos, and any links you open outside the app are provided by third parties. OmniToolkit does not control their privacy practices and is not responsible for them. When you play a stream, the station's server is contacted directly and the station's own privacy policy applies to that connection.
